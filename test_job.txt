Job Title: Senior AI Systems Engineer

- Collaborate with product designers and frontend teams to deliver delightful, enterprise-grade AI tools.

- 4+ years of professional software engineering experience with strong proficiency in Python and modern frameworks (FastAPI, Pydantic).
- Deep hands-on experience building generative AI applications with LLMs (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Claude).

- Strong grounding in software engineering best practices: clean architecture, typing, automated testing, and code quality.

Preferred Qualifications & Soft Skills:
